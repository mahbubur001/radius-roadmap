What makes “Transit” different

  • The roadmap is a line, not columns. Features travel Now → Next → Later → Shipped along one line drawn in the customer's brand colour. Desktop: left to right. Phone: top to bottom, so there is nothing to switch, you just scroll the journey.
  • Numbers are the hero. Votes, counts and dates are set big in a monospace face, like a departure board. People see demand at a glance.
  • Details open as a ticket: perforated edge, a status stamp, a big vote button, the ideas it came from, and the conversation.
  • Ideas are a board, not cards: one clean ledger with a vote column, status in small caps and look-alikes while you type.
  • Updates read like a journal: large dates in the margin, and every post shows what it shipped from the roadmap and how many people asked for it.
  • Help is a numbered index, like a field guide, with search first.
  • Phones get a bottom dock with a centre “Share an idea” button, reachable with one thumb.
  • White-label by design: only the line, the vote button and focus states take the brand colour; everything else is ink and paper, so any brand colour looks right. Light and dark.
