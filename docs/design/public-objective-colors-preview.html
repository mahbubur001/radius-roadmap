Where the colour goes (all options)

  • Roadmap cards: the small-caps objective above the title.
  • Topic chips above the line (one per objective).
  • Card ticket: the objective in the meta line under the title.
  • Ideas list: the idea's objective next to its status (ideas can have an objective too).
  • Not touched: the line, stations, vote buttons and stamps stay in the brand colour; status squares keep the status colours.
  • Dark mode: colours are the same; B mixes the tint with the dark sheet so text stays readable.
  • My pick: A. It adds the meaning (which objective) without competing with the brand colour, and it matches the status squares already on the Ideas page. B is louder and fights the brand when colours clash; C is the strongest, but four colours on one line of cards gets busy.
