“Signal”: design direction

Our product turns what customers say into shipped work, then tells them. The design is built on that one journey, not on a generic dashboard kit.

Signature

  • The stage tick: six hairline bars on every idea, card and search result show how far it has travelled, from New to Announced. You can read the whole product at a glance.
  • The Signal line on Home: the funnel from ideas to announced, with a nudge when something shipped but nobody was told (“close the loop”).

Layout

  • A workspace rail, a dimmed sidebar, and one inset “sheet” that holds the work. Navigation steps back so content leads (Linear's 2026 refresh: “don't compete for attention you haven't earned”).
  • Feedback is a three-pane triage view with keyboard shortcuts (J/K, S, M, E). Roadmap cards open in a side drawer, so you never lose your place.
  • ⌘K jumps anywhere: try it. Home is a “Needs you” queue, not vanity charts.

Colour

  • Warm graphite neutrals (not cold blue-grey, not cream). Primary buttons are ink. Colour is kept for meaning: the 6 stages, plus one signal accent your customers can rebrand.
newreviewplannedprogressshippedannounced

Type

  • Schibsted Grotesk for headings and big numbers, Geist for UI text, Geist Mono for ids, counts and meta.

Operator console

  • The same system in a “control room” setting: always-dark sheet, amber for Pro and warnings, and a health meter per account. Open it with the shield icon in the rail.

Try

  • Click the sidebar, the Signal stages, ideas in Feedback, cards on Roadmap, brand colours in Settings. Press ⌘K. Toggle dark mode at the bottom-left.
