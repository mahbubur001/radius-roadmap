Mobile rules behind these screens

  • Compact header: logo + name + sign-in in one 52px row; module tabs underneath scroll sideways and stay pinned.
  • Roadmap, one column at a time: column chips (with counts) or swipe; no sideways-scrolling board on a phone.
  • Thumb-first: vote on the left of each card, primary actions full-width at the bottom, floating “Share an idea”.
  • Sheets, not new pages: card and idea details open as a bottom sheet over the list; back/close keeps your place.
  • Tap targets ≥ 44px, inputs at 16px so iOS doesn't zoom, safe-area padding under the home bar.
  • Look-alike ideas while typing so people vote instead of posting duplicates (same search as the dashboard's duplicates).
  • White-label stays: the customer's brand colour drives buttons, active tab, votes and links; light/dark follows the site setting.
  • Embeds and widget use the same mobile components at their container width.
